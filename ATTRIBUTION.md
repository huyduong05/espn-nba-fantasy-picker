# Data Attribution

The NCAA men's basketball raw snapshots downloaded by this project are produced
by [SportsDataverse](https://github.com/sportsdataverse/ncaa-mbb-hoops-data) from
`stats.ncaa.org` records and distributed through
[`sportsdataverse-data`](https://github.com/sportsdataverse/sportsdataverse-data)
under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The project stores unmodified raw snapshots and will document transformations in
later phases. No endorsement by SportsDataverse or the NCAA is implied.

The historical injury archive is Randall Hopkins's
[NBA Injuries from 2010-2020](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ghopkins/nba-injuries-2010-2018),
version 3. Its Kaggle metadata declares the dataset `CC0: Public Domain`; the
publisher states that the records were scraped from Pro Sports Transactions.

NBA.com season-stat snapshots are governed by the
[NBA.com Terms of Use](https://www.nba.com/termsofuse), not an open-data license.
They are retained for the user's local educational project with that distinction
recorded in their manifests. No endorsement by the NBA is implied.
